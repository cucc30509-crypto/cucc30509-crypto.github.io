04 / Suggested Introduction

Moving customer information into a CRM is only one part of implementation. Your team also needs to know what to record, who owns each next step, and how the system fits into daily work.

For a small business, a useful starting point is one clearly defined workflow. This guide walks through planning that workflow, preparing your data, testing a rollout, and helping people use the system consistently—before expanding to more complex processes.

05 / Questions Worth Answering

  • What should a small business do before implementing a CRM?
  • Who should own the implementation process?
  • What information should be cleaned before migration?
  • How can a team test a CRM before a wider rollout?
  • What should CRM onboarding include?
  • How can a manager identify adoption problems?

Answers should use concrete examples and acknowledge that scope, timing, and migration complexity vary. Avoid a universal rollout deadline or unsupported cost estimate.
