The task

A support queue works better when each message has a clear category, priority, next action, and consistent response style. This sample focuses on written support rather than phone-based service.

Triage framework

Category First action Escalate when
General question Answer from verified FAQ or documentation. The answer is missing or policy-sensitive.
Order / account issue Confirm the information needed to investigate. Account access or payment action requires authorization.
Complaint Acknowledge the issue, summarize it, and state the next step. Refund, legal, safety, or high-risk decisions are involved.
Technical problem Gather device, error, timing, and reproduction details. Troubleshooting exceeds the approved support playbook.

Response principles

  • Answer the actual question before adding extra information.
  • Do not promise refunds, deadlines, or outcomes without authorization.
  • Use the company’s approved policy and tone.
  • Ask only for information needed to move the case forward.
  • Keep internal notes separate from the customer-facing response.
  • Escalate uncertainty instead of improvising policy.

Daily handoff

A short end-of-day update can summarize open cases, escalations, repeated issues, and anything blocking responses. That keeps the queue transparent without requiring a long meeting.
