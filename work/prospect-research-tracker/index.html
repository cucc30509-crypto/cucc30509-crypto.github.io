Prospect Research Tracker

A clean workflow for turning scattered public leads into a reviewable, follow-up-ready tracker.

Portfolio workflow sample — This is an independent demonstration created to show process and output quality. It does not contain private client data or claim commissioned results.

01 / Web Research & Lead Tracking

Make every lead traceable before outreach starts.

The task

A prospect list becomes useful only when another person can understand where each lead came from, what they need, why they may be relevant, and what happens next. This sample organizes that information into one reviewable workflow.

Suggested tracker

Field Purpose
Prospect / Company Identify the person or business clearly.
Source / Link Keep the original public source available for verification.
What they need Summarize the actual request instead of copying a vague job title.
Fit Record why the opportunity matches the service or role.
Date contacted Prevent duplicate outreach.
Reply / Status Separate no reply, interested, follow-up, and closed states.
Follow-up Set a specific next-action date.
Budget / Notes Preserve useful context without cluttering the main status fields.

Research workflow

  1. Find recent public opportunities from relevant communities or job sources.
  2. Open the original post and verify that the need is still clear and actionable.
  3. Capture the source URL before drafting outreach.
  4. Summarize requirements in plain language.
  5. Flag missing details such as budget, location restrictions, or required tools.
  6. Draft a personalized message only after the lead is recorded.
  7. Update the tracker after every reply or follow-up.

Quality checks

  • Remove duplicate URLs and repeated prospects.
  • Do not invent budgets, company details, or contact information.
  • Prefer primary posts over reposts or scraped listings.
  • Record uncertainty instead of guessing.
  • Keep outreach status separate from lead quality.
