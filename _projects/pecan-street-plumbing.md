## 01 / Current Page

[Source page: Pecan Street Plumbing services](https://www.callpecan.com/services)

**Brief-supplied baseline:** a broad services page with a generic “Services” H1 and relatively thin emergency plumbing content. These are audit hypotheses to verify against the current page, not quoted findings from a live crawl.

## 02 / Key Issues

| Priority | Audit question                                     | Recommendation                                                                               |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| High     | Does the page answer an urgent local need quickly? | Give emergency plumbing its own useful landing page, if this service and area are confirmed. |
| High     | Does “Services” explain the page’s purpose?        | Keep the overview broad; use a precise emergency-focused H1 on the proposed page.            |
| High     | Can a visitor understand the next action?          | Place a clear contact CTA beside confirmed availability and service-area information.        |
| Medium   | Is emergency guidance sufficiently useful?         | Explain problems handled, service boundaries, and how to request help.                       |

## 03 / SEO Opportunity

**Proposed direction: Emergency Plumber in Downtown Austin, TX.** Separate broad service browsing from urgent local intent. Confirm actual Downtown Austin coverage, emergency availability, licensing claims, and booking workflow before publishing. Do not create near-identical neighborhood pages.

“Why Downtown Austin Is Different” should contain verified operational details, such as building access or parking considerations. Remove it if the business cannot supply meaningful local information. Confirm every listed service before including it.

## 05 / Recommended Changes

1. **Verify the baseline.** Record the live title, meta description, headings, canonical, and actual emergency service offering.
2. **Choose a page role.** Keep the services hub as an overview. Create one dedicated emergency page only if it adds distinct value.
3. **Write for decisions.** Explain what information a caller should provide, what the team handles, and how availability is confirmed. Do not promise 24/7 or same-day arrival without evidence.
4. **Connect the pages.** Link from the hub using “emergency plumbing in Downtown Austin.” Link back to relevant pipe, drain, and water-heater services where those pages exist.
5. **Make the CTA accurate.** Use “Schedule Emergency Plumbing Service” only with a working scheduling flow; otherwise use “Contact the team to check availability.”
6. **Review before launch.** Check mobile readability, contact links, factual claims, unique metadata, and the canonical URL. Later measurement would require the owner’s actual analytics access.
